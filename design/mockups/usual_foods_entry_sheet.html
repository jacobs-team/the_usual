Usual foods — the add / edit meal sheet

Same sheet you have today, with one new control: a Whole meal / By foods toggle. "Whole meal" is exactly today's behavior — a meal with its own nutrition, no parts. "By foods" turns the sheet into a composition builder. You edit a food's nutrition by tapping it open (right phone); you never hand-edit a composed meal's total — it's the sum of its foods.

Whole meal — today's behavior, kept
Toggle on "Whole meal" and the sheet is what you have now: a name and one set of nutrition fields. No foods. Standalone meals are fully intact — nothing is removed.

Editing one food's nutrition
Tap a food's name to open its editor: the per-unit macro fields, plus "Save as a usual food" so it joins your library. This is how you correct a food's numbers — once, on the base unit.
