Usual foods — how composition shows up in the log

The hero moment: a logged usual meal made of foods, on the Today view. Tap-the-usual-then-done still works (collapsed, it's one line). The new power is expanding it to nudge one component — "chicken ×4 → ×3" — and watch the meal total recalc. Three directions for that interaction, with today's flat blob meal on the left for reference.

A · Nested accordion
Expand the meal → its foods appear as a nested mini-list. Each food is itself expandable with its own stepper. Reuses the exact row you already have, one level down. Powerful, but two levels of chevrons can feel deep.

B · Inline food list (recommended)
Expand the meal → a flat, compact list of its foods, each with an inline ＋/− stepper right there. No second chevron level: nudge ×4→×3 in one tap and the meal total above updates. Tap a food's name to edit its macros. Meal stays the hero; foods are light rows.

C · Drill-in detail
The day stays clean — composed meals show only as one line. Tap to push a full "meal detail" screen: total pinned at top, every food a full-width editable card. Best for big meals, but it's a navigation away from the day, more taps for a quick tweak.
