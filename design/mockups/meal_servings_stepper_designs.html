“Meal 12345” eaten twice (×2 servings). One serving = 386 cal · 9P · 1C · 34F, so the meal and Lunch totals read 772 cal · 18P · 2C · 68F. Ingredient rows always describe ONE serving.

Ingredients each have a stepper but the whole meal has none — no way to say “I had two of these”. Meal quantity is stuck at 1.

A · Servings row on top (recommended)
A SERVINGS stepper sits between the header and the ingredient group — same spot the simple-meal card puts QUANTITY. Ingredients below read as “what’s in one serving”. Collapsed header gains a ×2 marker.
